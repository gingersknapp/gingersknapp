## Hi! I'm Olivia Knapp

I'm a master’s student of Environmental Data Science.  I seeks to leverage ecological research and modeling to understand the interactions between plants, animals, water, and disturbance that shape California’s landscapes. When I'm not contemplating California’s oak savannahs and trade-networks pre European-contact, or illustrating local flora and fauna, I enjoy gardening and learning new crafting skills.

Pronouns: She/They

### What I'm doing now
- learning how to use data science skills for ecology research
- illustrating family-oriented field guides to California wildlife.

### Projects I have worked on:
- Investigating how urbanization changes acorn woodpecker social behavior, resource use, and reproductive success
- Exploring the trade-offs between plant adaptations to fire and herbivory in South African savannahs
- Biodiversity collections in north-easter South Africa (Limpopo)

### I'm currently learning:
- how to use R and Python for environmental data,
- geospatial analysis,
- how to crochet (with ambitions to bead croche native snakes),
- how to paint night-time scenes in watercolor

### Things that fascinate me
- How beavers shape fire-resilience
- How grasslands and oaks shape water avialability

### Special skills
- locating acorn woodpeckers within 150 meters
- tree-house building
- Defending lab space from monkeys with a slingshot
